# AXIOM backlink plan

## Objective

Earn relevant references to the public AXIOM homepage from people who work with school and college results. Prioritize qualified visitors and useful product discovery over a target number of links. The brand is AXIOM by Keystone Labs, described as academic performance analytics for schools and colleges.

This is a strategy, not a claim that outreach or links have already been obtained. No messages, directory submissions, paid placements or other-repository edits are authorized by this document alone.

## First month

### Week 1: accurate owned profiles

Review the Keystone Labs product listing, AXIOM GitHub repository description/website field, and the founder's existing professional profiles. Where the owner approves, use one direct HTTPS link to `https://axiom.keystonelabs.site/` and consistent product naming. Verify existing links before editing them. These are useful discovery paths; owner-controlled profiles should not be presented as independent endorsements.

### Week 2: publish something worth referencing

Prepare one practical, public resource based on AXIOM's actual behavior: a guide to comparing school sections fairly, or a checklist for cleaning student result workbooks. Explain missing marks and absence honestly. Use a synthetic workbook and invented student identities rather than institution data. Link the resource from the homepage and add its canonical URL to the sitemap only after publishing it. A useful future slug is `/guides/compare-student-results`; it does not exist yet.

Prepare a short product demonstration showing workbook import, section comparisons and reporting. Review all claims against the current app. Publish only to accounts the owner authorizes.

### Week 3: targeted institutional relationships

Build a small reviewed prospect list from actual pilot institutions, teacher-training groups, school-management communities, relevant university incubators and EdTech event organizers. Look for a genuine resource page, pilot write-up or case-study opportunity. Do not invent partnerships, scrape mailing lists or ask institutions to expose student reports.

After explicit permission to contact each destination, offer the relevant resource or a demonstration. Ask for a link only where it helps their readers. Prefer ordinary branded anchors such as “AXIOM” or “AXIOM by Keystone Labs”; let editors choose their own wording. No mass mailouts or guaranteed-link packages.

### Week 4: permissioned case study and measurement

If an institution agrees, publish a case study describing the problem, process and verified outcome. Obtain permission before naming the institution or using screenshots. Invite the institution to reference it from its own site if useful. Do not manufacture time-savings figures or testimonials.

Record each opportunity with source URL, relationship, owner permission, contact status, target page, link status and referral outcomes. Track Search Console impressions/clicks and meaningful inquiries over time. A small number of relevant editorial references is more useful than indiscriminate directory volume; no ranking guarantee is implied.

## Boundaries

Do not purchase ranking links, use private blog networks, automate comment links, exchange links at scale, or require keyword-rich links as a condition of service. Qualify legitimate sponsored placements appropriately. Avoid making every Keystone Labs product link to every other product solely for SEO.

Reference: [Google link spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam).
